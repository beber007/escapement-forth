What this page is, and is not

  • The Forth and the kernel are the board's own code, compiled unchanged: the console and the pool of slots (src/), the hard kernel of Escapement under EDF (EscapementHard.c) with the four tasks of the Pico, one a period. Only the layer under them differs (web/platform_web.c, web/kernel/).
  • The console is the kernel's idle task here: the slots preempt it, and one another, wherever the Forth reads the clock, every sixteen words. On the board the console has a core of its own, and never delays a slot.
  • The kernel's clock follows the browser's, which lets the page run every few milliseconds, seldom in a tab left in the background. The time it held the page, the clock passes release by release, each on time for the kernel, late on the wall; past 200 ms, the rest is not given at all.
  • The times slots shows are those of your browser, not of the Pico, read to a tenth of a millisecond, or a whole one, depending on the browser: a short word reads 0. Nothing here tells the costs of the kernel on a chip, nor its energy.
  • A line typed at the console is stopped after 10 s, as Ctrl-C does on the board. The dictionary lives as long as the page; save has no flash to write.
